Section 2
Hiring Process
Days 1–2 · Three steps from first contact to offer accepted.
Days 1–2
1

Initial Interview

  • Duration: 30–45 minutes, virtual
  • Set expectations about the fully virtual work model early
  • Evaluate communication, coachability, and follow-through signals
  • If strong fit: invite to CLB same day or next morning
2

Career Leadership Briefing (CLB)

  • Group or virtual setting — runs independently of individual hiring
  • Build excitement and urgency around getting licensed
  • Connect candidates to existing agents so they see real proof
  • End by telling them the final interview is next
3

Final Interview & Commitment

  • Confirm the offer and role expectations
  • Introduce the schooling plan and daily chapter schedule
  • Identify which scholarship program applies (see Scholarships)
  • Cover the cost of pre-licensing — frame it as an investment, not a gift
  • Lock in the class date together on the call
  • Send enrollment link within 1 hour of hanging up
⚠ FRAMING THAT WORKS "We're investing in you before you've proven yourself — because that's how you build a real team. In return, I need your commitment to finish within 14 days."
💰 SCHOLARSHIP REMINDER Remind them: finish within 15 days of enrollment = 100% reimbursement on the next paycheck. Use this as a closing motivator.

Section 4
TNT Classroom Training
Days 17–21 · Licensed and ready to build.
Days 17–21

Once the agent passes their state exam, they move immediately into TNT training. No waiting — the goal is to get them from licensed to earning as fast as possible.

  • 5 days of classroom-style virtual training
  • Agent arrives with license in hand — no delays, no exceptions
  • Reinforce daily check-ins throughout TNT to maintain momentum
  • Celebrate the licensing milestone publicly within the team
⚠ FRAMING TIP"Get licensed, and you're in training the following week. Training is where you start building your income."

Section 8
Trust-Building Strategies
Six practices that close the trust gap with virtual agents.

Most trust breakdowns happen because agents don't believe the opportunity is real, or feel unsupported once they're on their own. These six practices close the gap.

🗓 Lock in the class date at offer

Pick the date together during the final interview. A calendar commitment shifts psychology from "I'll think about it" to "this is real."

📋 Send chapter schedule in writing

Share the daily outline right after the final interview. Removes ambiguity — they know exactly what 14 days looks like.

💬 Daily progress check-ins

A brief morning message keeps momentum and shows you're invested in their success, not just waiting on a license.

📞 Introduce the Concierge Team

Name the Concierge Team during the final interview and Day 1 message. Normalizes reaching out when stuck.

🏆 Celebrate milestones

Acknowledge when they finish the live class, pass a practice test, and schedule the exam. Small wins build trust fast.

👥 Connect them to teammates

Introduce them to other agents during or after the CLB. A peer community proves the opportunity is real.

Section 9
Final Interview Script
Use as a guide, not word-for-word. Adapt to your voice.
Step 1 — Open With Congratulations
You (Manager)
"[Name], I'm really glad you're here. You've made it through the initial interview and the Career Leadership Briefing, and I want you to know — we don't bring everyone to this point. The fact that you're here tells me you have what it takes. So today, we're going to finalize everything and get you set up to launch."
📌 TONEWarm, direct, celebratory. This is an offer, not another evaluation. They should feel chosen.
Step 2 — Confirm the Role & Virtual Model
You (Manager)
"Let me be upfront about how we work. Our team is fully virtual — you'll never need to commute to an office. That's a real advantage, but it also means we rely on each other to follow through. When you say you'll do something, I need to know it's done. That's the foundation of how we operate together."
Step 3 — Schooling, Scholarship & Investment
You (Manager)
"To get started, you'll need your insurance license — that's non-negotiable in this business. Here's what I want you to hear: we're going to cover the cost of your pre-licensing coursework. That's on us. And through our scholarship program, once you pass your exam, we reimburse you. Finish in 15 days or less — you get 100% back on your next paycheck. 16 to 30 days is 50% back. So the faster you finish, the more you get back."
You (Manager)
"In return, what I need from you is your commitment. We're going to get you licensed in 7 to 14 days. I'll be with you every step of the way — daily check-ins, answers to questions, cheering you on. But I need you to show up for yourself every single day. Can you do that?"
Them (Expected)
"Yes, absolutely."
Step 4 — Introduce the Agent Concierge Team
You (Manager)
"One more thing — you're not doing this alone. Globe Life has a dedicated Agent Concierge Team whose entire job is to support new agents through exam prep. If you're ever stuck, confused, or not sure what to do next, reach out to them: LNAgentConcierge@Globe.Life or call or text 469-317-0281. They'll answer questions, connect you to resources, and help you stay on track. Use them."
Step 5 — Walk Through the Chapter Schedule
You (Manager)
"Let me show you exactly what this looks like — because I don't want you staring at a course wondering what to do. Here's the schedule I use with all my agents. You'll cover one to two chapters per day. [State course provider] runs a live virtual class first, then you use the practice tests and flash cards to lock it in. By Day 14, you sit for your state exam — and then we get you into TNT training the following week."
📌 NOTEShare the chapter schedule (Pre-Licensing section) here. Walk through it briefly.
Step 6 — Lock In the Class Date Right Now
You (Manager)
"Alright, let's get your class date on the calendar right now while we're together. Pull up your calendar — I'll wait. What's the soonest you can commit to starting? Ideally this week or early next week."
📌 CRITICALDo not end this call without a class date on the calendar.
You (Manager)
"Perfect. So you'll start on [date]. I'll send you the enrollment link right after this call. You've got your schedule, you've got the Concierge Team, you've got my support — and we've got your start date. This is really happening."
Step 7 — Close Strong
You (Manager) — Close
"[Name], I am genuinely excited for you. You're about to do something most people never follow through on — they think about getting licensed, they talk about it, but they don't actually do it. You're doing it. In three weeks, you're going to be a licensed agent, in TNT training, and building something real. I'll be reaching out tonight with your enrollment link and your daily schedule. Welcome to the team."
Objection Handling
"I'm not sure I can finish in 14 days — I have [job / kids / schedule]."
"I hear you — life is busy. That's exactly why we built a one-to-two chapter per day plan. It's about 60–90 minutes of focused study. You don't have to quit your job or rearrange your life — you just have to protect that hour or two each day. Most agents with full schedules get it done in 10–12 days. What part of your schedule worries you most?"
"Can I think about the start date and get back to you?"
"Of course — but every week we delay is a week you're not earning. And remember: the faster you finish, the more the scholarship covers. I don't want 'I'll think about it' to cost you the 100% reimbursement window. What would help you feel ready to commit to a date today?"
"What if I don't pass the exam?"
"Great question — the course has a strong pass rate when you complete it and use the practice tests. If you don't pass on the first try, we'll work through it together. The Concierge Team can help you identify weak areas and get rescheduled. This isn't a one-shot deal. Follow the daily plan and you'll be ready."

Section 10
Daily Check-In Messages
14 days of messages. Tone: coach texting an athlete — warm, direct, invested.
Pre-Class Prep · Days 1–2
Day 1 Evening — within 1 hour of final interview
"Hey [Name]! So great to officially have you on the team. Here's your [TSI National / Xcel Solutions] enrollment link: [link]. Get signed up tonight so you're ready to roll. Your class starts [date] — I'll be checking in every morning to keep you on track. If you ever get stuck during prep, the Agent Concierge Team is there: LNAgentConcierge@Globe.Life or 469-317-0281. Let's get you licensed!"

Send within 1 hour of hanging up. Include the Concierge contact info.

Day 2 Morning — confirm enrollment
"Good morning [Name]! Quick check — did you get enrolled last night? If you ran into any issues, let me know. Your class is [X days] away — browse the course dashboard so it's familiar when class begins. Today's goal: enrolled and logged in."

If not enrolled yet — this is your early warning. Address now, not Day 5.

Live Virtual Class · Days 3–4
Day 3 Morning — class day 1
"Today's the day! Your class kicks off this morning — log in 5 minutes early. [Illinois agents: webcam must be on the entire time and you must answer all polling questions or you'll have to reschedule.] Have a notepad ready, ask questions, soak everything in. Text me tonight!"

Illinois: Remind IL agents of webcam and polling question requirements explicitly.

Day 4 Morning — class day 2
"Day 2 of class — halfway through the hardest part. Today covers health insurance and state regulations. Pay extra attention to the state law section — it always shows up on the exam. Almost done with the live portion!"
Self-Study & Review · Days 5–12
Day 5
"Great work finishing the live class! Now the real prep begins. Today: life insurance basics — use the flash cards in the portal. Goal: 80%+ on the practice quiz before moving on. How's it feeling?"
Day 6
"Good morning! Today: policy types and riders — term, whole, universal. These show up a lot on the exam. Pro tip: quiz yourself out loud, don't just read definitions. Let me know your practice score tonight."
Day 7 — One week in!
"One week in — you're doing great! Today is annuities. Key thing: an annuity is the opposite of life insurance — life insurance protects against dying too soon, an annuity protects against living too long. Once that clicks, the chapter gets easy."
Day 8
"Today: group insurance — shorter chapter. Use the extra time to revisit any life insurance topics that felt fuzzy. The exam loves to combine concepts, so strong fundamentals matter more than memorizing every detail."
Day 9
"Health chapter today — this is where people slow down, so push through. Focus on deductibles, copays, coinsurance, and out-of-pocket maximums. Those four concepts show up constantly. How many practice questions are you hitting daily?"
Day 10
"Almost in the home stretch! Today: HMOs, PPOs, Medicare, and Medicaid. Know the difference between Medicare Part A, B, C, and D — it's a favorite on the exam. Two days from full review. Stay focused."
Day 11
"Today: state law and ethics — usually 10–15% of the actual exam, so don't skip it. This is the last new content chapter. Tomorrow is all review. The finish line is close."
Day 12 — Full review day
"Full review day — no new chapters, just reinforcement. Take a full practice exam today: no notes, timer on. Anything under 75% in a topic area, go back to those flash cards. You're not cramming — you're confirming what you already know."
Exam Week · Days 13–14
Day 13 — Schedule the exam
"Today's mission: schedule your state exam at pearsonvue.com. [Illinois: remember the mandatory 5-day wait — make sure fingerprinting is done this week.] Then one more light review pass. Light study today, confidence tomorrow."

Illinois: Remind IL agents of the 5-day wait and fingerprinting requirement.

Day 14 — Exam day
"[Name] — today is the day. You've studied, you've shown up every single day, and you are ready. Arrive early with valid government-issued photo ID. Breathe, and trust what you've learned. Text me as soon as you're done. Go get that license!"

PASS: respond immediately and warmly. DON'T PASS: "Let's review your diagnostic, identify weak areas, and get you rescheduled. This is not the end. The Concierge Team can help too: 469-317-0281."

Licensed! · Day 15+
Day 15 — After passing
"CONGRATULATIONS [Name]!!! You did it — you are now a licensed insurance agent. Submit your passing results to the Concierge Team right away for your scholarship reimbursement: LNAgentConcierge@Globe.Life or 469-317-0281. Here's what's next: [TNT training dates, logistics]. Get some rest — because Monday, we're building your business. Welcome to the team. For real this time."
