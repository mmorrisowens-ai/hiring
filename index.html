Recruiting Center

Candidate to Hire

Use this section when you need to move a candidate from interest to commitment without muddying the process.

Hiring Flow
StepPurposeManager Move
Initial InterviewFind fit, why, communication, coachability.Invite strong candidate to CLB.
Career Leadership BriefingShow opportunity, culture, compensation, and proof.Book final interview immediately.
Final InterviewConfirm commitment, income lag factor, schedule expectations.Offer role and lock school/class date.
School EnrollmentMove from verbal yes to action.Send link within 1 hour and confirm enrollment.
Final Interview Offer Script
Manager
[Name], I’m really glad you’re here. You’ve made it through the initial interview and the Career Leadership Briefing, and we don’t bring everyone to this point. Today, we’re going to finalize everything and get you set up to launch.
Commitment
To get started, you’ll need your insurance license. We’re going to help you get through that process, but I need your commitment to show up daily. The goal is to get licensed quickly, get into training, and start building income.
Key RuleDo not end the final interview without a class date on the calendar. A date is a commitment.
Ghosted Candidate Recovery
Text
Hey [Name], I wanted to check in. We missed you for your final interview. I know life happens, so I’m not assuming anything negative. Are you still interested in moving forward, or should I open that spot for the next candidate?
Voicemail
Hey [Name], this is [Manager]. We had your final interview scheduled and I didn’t see you come through. I wanted to give you the chance to reconnect before I move forward with other candidates. Send me a quick text either way.

Hire to Exam

Everything a manager needs to guide a new hire through pre-licensing, school, exam prep, and reimbursement.

14-Day Licensing Plan
DaysFocusManager Check
1–2Enrollment, login, calendar block.Confirm they can access the course.
3–4Live virtual class / webinar.Remind them to log in early and stay fully present.
5–12Self-study, flash cards, practice exams.Ask for practice scores and weak areas.
13–14Schedule exam, final review, exam day.Send confidence text and post-exam follow-up.
15+Pass results, reimbursement, TNT dates.Celebrate publicly and move them into training.
Scholarship Rules

0–15 Days

100% reimbursement on next available paycheck after passing results are submitted.

16–30 Days

50% reimbursement. Use the 15-day window as urgency during the final interview.

Program A

Permanent license states, including Missouri and Illinois.

Program B

Temp-to-permanent states, if applicable.

State Requirements
StateProviderImportant Notes
MissouriTSI NationalPermanent license required. Pearson VUE in-person only. No fingerprints for Life/Health.
IllinoisXcel SolutionsMandatory live webinar. Webcam on. Pearson VUE in-person only. Fingerprints required. 5 business day wait after passing before NIPR application.
ReminderAlways verify state fees and requirements before quoting them. Use the state DOI or course provider if something seems off.

The Talk — Accountability Framework
Purpose
The purpose of this conversation is to make sure you clearly understand what comes next, what I expect from you, and what you should expect from me.
Commitment
I am 100% committed to your training and success. But my 100% is only half of what you need. The other half has to come from you.
Accountability
If I hold you accountable, it is because I care about your success. Is there ever going to be a time where you hold the fact that I care about your success against me?

Diagnose and Coach

Use this when production is not where it needs to be and you need a clean coaching path.

Coaching Diagnostics
IssueCheck FirstCoach Toward
Low Appointment RateDials, contact rate, script adherence, ask rate.More attempts, stronger opener, better transition to appointment.
Low Show RateConfirmation process, urgency, reminder cadence.Better appointment framing and same-day reminders.
Low Closing RatioFact finder, emotional connection, need development, close sequence.Slow down discovery and connect recommendation to stated need.
Low Referral CountDid they ask? How many? Did they roll referrals immediately?Ask confidently for 7–10 and call while energy is high.

Power + Pivot Examples
I'm busy
Totally fair — most of the people I help are busy. That’s actually why I’m calling briefly: to make sure this doesn’t take more time than it needs to. Would later today or tomorrow be less disruptive?
I'm all set
I hear you. When you say you’re all set, is that because you recently reviewed everything and know nothing has changed, or more that you’re not looking to deal with insurance today?
I can't afford it
I completely understand. Just so I don’t assume, when you say you can’t afford it, is that based on what you had before, or have you actually seen what the options would look like now?
